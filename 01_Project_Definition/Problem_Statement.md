# Problem Statement

Many university students experience difficulties when organizing their studies due to different learning preferences, changing levels of motivation, and study-related pressure.

Traditional study planning applications mainly focus on schedules, reminders, and task management. However, these applications may not fully consider individual learning preferences, study progress, and students' voluntarily provided mood feedback.

As a result, students may find it difficult to select suitable learning activities and maintain a study plan that matches their current needs.

Therefore, MindMate AI is proposed as an emotion-aware AI study assistant that aims to provide personalized study recommendations and learning activities based on students' voluntarily provided mood feedback, learning preferences, and study progress.

# Proposed Solution

MindMate AI aims to provide the following features:

* **Mood-Based Study Suggestions:** Recommend suitable study activities based on students' voluntarily selected moods.
* **Personalized Study Plans:** Create study plans according to individual learning preferences and goals.
* **Interactive Quizzes:** Provide quizzes to make learning more engaging.
* **Learning Assistance:** Help students understand academic topics.
* **Progress Tracking:** Allow students to monitor their study progress.
* **Study Reminders:** Remind students about planned learning activities.

# Expected Benefits

* Supports personalized learning experiences.
* Helps students organize their study activities.
* Encourages interactive learning.
* Helps students monitor their academic progress.
* Provides study recommendations based on individual preferences.

# Important Consideration

Mood selection is completely voluntary. MindMate AI is an educational support application and does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
