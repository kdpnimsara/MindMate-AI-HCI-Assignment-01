# MindMate AI

## Your Emotion-Aware AI Study Companion

**Module:** Human Computer Interaction
**Assignment:** Assignment 01 – New Product Experience Design
**Product Type:** AI-Based Mobile Application

## 1. Project Overview

MindMate AI is a proposed AI-powered mobile application designed to improve the learning experience of university students.

The application aims to provide a personalized study experience by considering students' voluntarily provided mood feedback, learning preferences, and study progress.

Based on this information, MindMate AI proposes suitable study activities, learning recommendations, and study plans to support students during their academic journey.

## 2. Problem / Opportunity

University students may experience difficulties when planning their studies and selecting suitable learning activities.

A single study method may not be suitable for every student or every study situation. Students may also need support when they feel stressed, bored, tired, or less motivated to study.

MindMate AI aims to address this opportunity by providing personalized study recommendations based on students' preferences and voluntarily provided feedback.

## 3. Main Goal

To design a user-friendly and personalized study experience that helps university students organize their learning activities and manage their study progress effectively.

## 4. Main Features

1. Student Registration and Login
2. Mood Selection
3. AI-Based Study Recommendations
4. Personalized Study Planner
5. Interactive Quizzes
6. Learning Assistance
7. Study Progress Tracking
8. Study Reminders
9. Personalized Learning Activities
10. Student Profile Management

## 5. How MindMate AI Works

1. Students create an account and complete the onboarding process.
2. Students voluntarily select their current mood.
3. The application considers their learning preferences and study progress.
4. The AI system suggests suitable study activities and recommendations.
5. Students use the study planner and learning features.
6. The application tracks study progress and provides updated recommendations.

## 6. Value Proposition

MindMate AI aims to combine mood-based feedback, personalized study planning, and interactive learning activities in one mobile application.

Its main value is to help students choose suitable learning activities according to their current preferences and study progress.

## 7. Platform

Mobile Application

## 8. Proposed Target Users

* University Students
* Undergraduate Students
* Students who need personalized study planning
* Students who prefer interactive learning activities

**Note:** These are proposed target user groups. Actual user research, including surveys and interviews, will be conducted to identify and validate their needs, expectations, and pain points.

## 9. Important Design Consideration

Mood selection is voluntary. MindMate AI is designed as an educational support application and does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
