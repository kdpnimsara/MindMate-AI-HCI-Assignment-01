# MindMate AI – User Interview Guide

## Purpose

To understand university students' study habits, challenges, learning preferences, and expectations of a personalized AI study assistant.

## Interview Questions

1. How do you normally organize your studies?

2. What problems do you experience during studying?

3. What makes studying difficult for you?

4. How do you decide which subject or topic to study first?

5. What applications or digital tools do you currently use for studying?

6. What difficulties or limitations do you experience when using those tools?

7. What features would help you improve your study experience?

8. Would you use an application that suggests study activities based on your voluntarily provided feedback and learning preferences? Why?

9. How would you feel about voluntarily selecting your current study mood in an application?

10. What would make such an application easy and comfortable to use?

11. Do you have any additional suggestions for an AI-based study assistant?

## Research Ethics

* Participation must be voluntary.
* Obtain participant permission before recording responses.
* Do not collect unnecessary personal identifying information.
* Keep participant responses confidential.
* Record genuine responses without changing their meaning.
* Do not create fictional interview responses.
